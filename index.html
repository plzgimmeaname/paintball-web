How to Play

Every hit covers your opponent in paint. Fill their meter to 100% to take the round, or have less paint on you when the clock runs out.

Player 1 · Red

AD move

W jump (tap again to double jump)

S or Space shoot

Player 2 · Blue

←→ move

↑ jump (tap again to double jump)

↓ or Enter shoot

  • Against the CPU or online, either set of keys works for you.
  • Thin platforms can be jumped through from below.
  • Hold jump to go higher, tap for a short hop.
  • On City Ruins, falling into the gap paints you out instantly.
  • Esc pauses (or leaves an online match).
